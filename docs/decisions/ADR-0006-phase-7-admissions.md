# ADR-0006: School-owned admissions workflow and atomic conversion

**Status:** Accepted for Phase 7 implementation

**Context:** Schools need a staff workflow to capture enquiries and applications, track review decisions, and admit an accepted applicant. Admissions staff need to complete that work without receiving broad access to existing student, guardian, and enrollment records. Conversion must not leave a student or guardian profile behind when enrollment validation fails.

**Decision:** Store an admissions case and its guardian submissions under a tenant and school. Record every creation, edit, and workflow transition in case history. A single staff member with school-scoped Admissions management access may review and accept or reject; acceptance does not require a second approver. A separate `admissions.convert` permission controls the final admission action. Conversion calls the People and Enrollment contracts in the same tenant transaction: it creates or links student and guardian profiles and relationships, creates the school admission and initial academic placement, stores the resulting references on the case, and changes its status to `admitted`. Any validation or persistence failure rolls back the complete operation. The built-in Admission Officer role receives only Admissions read, manage, and convert permissions.

**Alternatives:** A public family portal, document uploads, and automatic emails would broaden the first release to include external identities, file storage, and delivery/retry behavior. Separate create-person and create-enrollment requests could leave partial records. Requiring a second reviewer would add a distinct approval policy and staffing dependency.

**Consequences:** Admissions remains a school-owned workflow while student, guardian, relationship, and enrollment data retain ownership in People and Enrollment. Existing identities can be linked by an explicit profile ID validated by the server; new identities use codes supplied at conversion. Applicants and guardian submissions remain attached to the case, and event history is preserved after rejection, withdrawal, or admission. Searchable cross-domain people selection, family access, uploads, and automatic email are deferred. The conversion transaction coordinates multiple domain contracts and must keep tenant context, scope checks, and rollback coverage intact.
